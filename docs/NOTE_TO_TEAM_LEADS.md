# Your team's AI usage — now inside the assistant

Hi all,

You asked to see how much your team is using our AI assistants, and what it
costs, without having to chase the platform team or open another dashboard. That
is live now. You can ask the assistant directly.

## What you can ask

Just ask in plain language. For example:

- *"How much did my team use last month?"*
- *"What did we spend on Claude vs Gemini in September?"*
- *"Who were my top five users last week?"*
- *"Is web search turned on for my team?"*

Behind those questions are four tools — `usage_summary`, `top_users`,
`capability_status` and (for admins) `set_capability` — but you don't need to
name them. "Last 7 days", "last 30 days", a specific month like `2026-09`, or a
specific week like `2026-W36` all work. Numbers are tokens (the unit both
platforms bill in) and US dollars, with Claude and Gemini broken out and added
together.

A note on dates: the figures run over our eight-week pilot window (early August
to late September 2026). "Last month" and "last week" are measured from the end
of that window, so you always see complete days.

## What you can see — and what you can't

- **You see your own group, in full.** Totals, the platform split, and the per-
  person leaderboard for your team.
- **You cannot see other groups.** If you ask for another team's usage, the
  assistant will tell you it can't — by design, not by accident. This is enforced
  in the service, so there is no phrasing that gets around it. You'll get a clear
  message, never another team's numbers.
- **Only the platform admins change capability controls** (the switches that
  enable or disable things like web search for a group). You can *see* your
  group's switches; changing them is an admin action, so there's an audit trail
  of who changed what and when.

This mirrors how the rest of our tooling works: you own your team's view, the
platform team owns the controls.

## If you need more

- **Want to see a group you don't own** (e.g. you've taken on a second team)?
  Reply here and we'll update your groups in the directory — access follows the
  directory, so once you're listed, the assistant will show it.
- **Want a capability turned on or off for your team** (web search, code
  execution)? Ask us and we'll flip the switch. We keep it off by default and
  turn things on per team as you ask — that keeps spend and risk predictable.
- **Think a number looks wrong?** Send us the question you asked and the period;
  the figures come straight from the Claude and Gemini usage exports, and we can
  trace any total back to the underlying days.

Thanks for pushing for this — it's a better loop for everyone when you can answer
"how much are we using?" yourselves.
